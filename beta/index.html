Questions

Is it really free?
The app is free. Every scene plays with a small watermark so you can try everything. Own a scene and it plays clean.
I already bought a RexAffects plugin. Do I pay again?
No. Sign in with the email you bought it with, and scenes you already own unlock.
Do I need Resolume?
No. The app runs the visuals itself. If you do use Resolume, you'll be able to send the output into it later.
Mac?
Windows first. Mac is being tested now.
It's a beta. What does that mean?
It works, and we're polishing it with the first players. Found a bug or have an idea? Tell us. That's the whole point.
