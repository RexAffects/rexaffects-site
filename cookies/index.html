We use cookies from Meta to see which of our ads work. Nothing else tracks you. This page is the detail behind the cookie notice, and part of our Privacy Policy (§2.1).

Your choice

Your choice on this browser: not asked yet.

Meta's pixel loads only after you press Allow. Until then nothing from Meta loads and no cookie is set. No thanks is remembered for 6 months and Allow for 12; after that we ask again. If your browser sends Global Privacy Control, we treat it as No thanks and never ask. The Cookies link at the bottom of every page brings the notice back. A pixel that has already loaded can't be unloaded from an open page, so changing Allow to No thanks takes effect from the next page you open.
