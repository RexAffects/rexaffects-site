Controller map: every button, every scene (the default)

This is what every button does the first time you load the player. You can change any of it on the CONTROLS tab (hover a row → EDIT → press a button), save it as a button layout and share it: see CONTROLLER.md. Your changes are a layer on top; RESET always brings this page back.

A row like R1 + ▲ is a chord: hold the first, press the second.

Tap fast = bigger steps. An arrow row that says a press (speed, energy, dissipation, a level) moves twice as far when you tap it again quickly (within about a third of a second); a slower tap is back to one step, and holding still glides at the normal rate. A (wraps) row (a palette, a flash mode) always moves one at a time. Same for ▲ ▼ ◀ ▶ in the FX channel.

Sticks rows are the analog sticks (PlayStation and Xbox; the 8BitDo Micro has none, so they never show for it). A sway springs back to the preset when you let go; a nudge stays. Neither is ever saved into a preset. They cannot be remapped yet (read-only on the CONTROLS tab).

Buttons are by position, the same on every controller: the right face button is ○ on PlayStation, B on Xbox, A on the 8BitDo Micro.

In the FX channel (the Effect Layer, the fluid over any scene): the four face buttons = the four fluid presets (the lit one again = layer off) · ◀ ▶ = previous / next favorite fluid preset · ▲ ▼ = fluid energy · hold L1 + ◀ ▶ = dissipation (▶ = the fluid fades faster) · hold energy or dissipation to glide · a new preset starts from its own energy and dissipation · R2 = next favorite fluid preset · hold R2 + ◀ ▶ = choose · hold L1 + ▲ ▼ = layer intensity ± 10 % · R1 = SEND SHOCK (one wave through the fluid, now) · hold R1 + ◀ ▶ = shock style (IRIS RING, OBJECT BURST out of the scene objects, …) · hold R1 + ▲ ▼ = shock strength · hold R1 1 s alone = shock PULSE on / off · tap L1 = SOURCE (AUTO → SCENE → YOU) · hold L1 1 s alone = LEARN BACKGROUND · L2 = the scene underneath, as always.

Lost? Get back: hold R2 for 1 second and let go = RESET SCENE: the scene goes back to its first favorite preset, clean (every latch, hold, trim and running sequence off, the Effect Layer off, the SCENE channel, the PARAMS shelf pad focus off). After 1 s a toast says LET GO = RESET SCENE; pressing ◀ ▶ instead still chooses a preset. Your camera in / out, LIGHT and AUDIO stay as you set them. Keyboard: 0. Same in the FX channel.

Music: hold L2 + ▲ = TAP TEMPO (4 taps on the beat; one tap after a pause = this is the ONE) · hold L2 + ▼ = AUDIO ON / OFF. Keyboard: T tap, Shift+T on / off. See AUDIO.md.

A face button the scene leaves free keeps a preset action: X (top) = next preset · B (bottom) = previous preset · Y (left) = reset preset. Keyboard: N / P scenes, , / . presets, Ctrl+P save preset, Ctrl+S save the show, Ctrl+Shift+R resets every control to this page.
