Use your phone as the camera

Plugin Player can use your phone as its live camera today. The phone reaches the app as an ordinary camera: you connect it with a cable or a free app, then pick it in CAM ▾. Every scene that uses a picture then runs on your phone's live view.

Inside the app: CAM ▾ → USE YOUR PHONE AS THE CAMERA has the same steps. Nothing downloads by itself, and nothing here is required: the demo clip and any webcam keep working without it.

The quickest way

iPhone on Windows: the free Camo app with its cable. Install Camo on the iPhone (App Store) and Camo Studio on the PC (camo.com). Plug in, tap Trust, open both. It is free at 720p with no watermark. If Camo Studio cannot see the iPhone, install Apple Devices from the Microsoft Store.

Android on Windows:

  1. Plug the phone in with a data cable, tap the USB notification on the phone, and look for Webcam. If it is there, pick it. You need no app at all, and it shows up as Android Webcam. Pixel phones have it. Android 14 or newer is required, and Samsung and Motorola are reported to leave it off.
  2. No Webcam option? On Windows 11, use Link to Windows: nothing to install on the PC. Install Link to Windows on the phone, sign in with the same Microsoft account, then on the PC go to Settings › Bluetooth & devices › Mobile devices, turn on Allow this PC to access your mobile devices, pick your phone, and turn on Use as connected camera. It needs Android 10 or newer and is wireless.

No internet where you play? DroidCam works with no internet: same Wi-Fi, the phone's hotspot, or USB. It runs on iPhone and Android, and the free version is standard definition (about 480p), which is plenty for the effects.

On a Mac, with Resolume, or with NDI: see the full table in CAMERA.md.

Then, every time

  1. Open CAM ▾. Plugin Player says PHONE CAMERA FOUND · USE IT? when it spots a phone. Press USE IT, or click it in the list. Not sure which row is the phone? It is the name you installed, or Windows Virtual Camera on Link to Windows.
  2. Press MEASURE DELAY, with the phone pointed at the screen. This flashes the screen 8 times: skip it if flashing light affects you or anyone who can see the screen.
  3. Under 200 ms feels live. 200–350 ms trails a little on fast moves. Over 350 ms, the effects trail behind dancers: use a cable, or get closer to the Wi-Fi.

At a show

  • Use a cable if you can. It charges the phone, and a crowd full of phones can't jam it.
  • No cable? Use the filming phone's own hotspot, on 5 GHz if it offers it.
  • Turn auto-lock off and plug in a charger or power bank.
  • Airplane mode with Wi-Fi back on stops a call from taking the camera.
  • Measure the delay again at the venue. It changes with the light and the Wi-Fi.
  • Test what happens if the phone drops before the show: close the phone app and watch the picture. A short hiccup just holds the last picture. After 3 seconds with nothing new, the chip reads NO PICTURE and the demo clip plays; it comes back by itself when the phone does. A pulled USB webcam-mode cable reads LOST and also comes back by itself. Some phone apps send their own logo when they lose the phone: Plugin Player cannot tell that from a picture, so it stays on the scene. Pick another camera in CAM ▾, or press −.

Free versions run 480–720p. The scenes use 720p at most, so a paid upgrade will not sharpen them much.

Coming later: scan a QR code with your phone and send its camera with no app to install. It is not in the beta.

If it does not show up

What you seeDo
The phone is not in CAM ▾Press ⟳. Check the phone app is open and the phone is unlocked
It is listed, the picture is black or a logoThe phone app is not sending: open it on the phone, check it is connected, keep the screen on
CAM BLOCKEDAnother app has the camera, usually the phone app's own preview, Zoom or Teams: close it, then V twice
An iPhone is not found over USBTap Trust, use an Apple cable, plug straight into the laptop, not a hub
The picture is backwardsCAM ▾ → PICTURE → MIRROR
