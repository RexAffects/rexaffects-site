Install in a minute

  1. Run . If your browser says the file "isn't commonly downloaded", choose Keep (in Edge: the … menu, Keep, then Keep anyway).
  2. Windows may show a blue box, "Windows protected your PC". That is normal for this beta: it isn't signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Plugin Player opens by itself, fullscreen, with a first scene playing. Plug in a controller and press a button.

On a Mac

  1. Open and drag Plugin Player into Applications.
  2. Open it from Applications. The first time, macOS asks if you want to open an app downloaded from the internet: click Open.
  3. Allow the camera if you want to use it. Plug in a controller and press a button.

Step by step, with what to do if Smart App Control blocks it: Install guide. Updates arrive by themselves after this.

For the technical folks: verify your download

Each file has a SHA-256 fingerprint. If yours matches, it's exactly the file we published.

Windows (PowerShell, in your Downloads folder):

Get-FileHash .\

          
Mac (Terminal, in Downloads): shasum -a 256 

          

        

      


      

        
What you need

        
    
          
  • Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) or macOS 13 or newer (Apple silicon or Intel), and about 400 MB of free disk space.
    • 
          
  • A graphics chip from the last several years (NVIDIA, AMD, Intel or Apple). A laptop with a dedicated GPU is best for the fluid scenes.
    • 
          
  • 8 GB of RAM or more.
    • 
          
  • A controller to play it: Xbox, PlayStation, 8BitDo and more, or your phone (scan the QR code in the app). Don't see yours working? Send a request with the Feedback/Requests button at the bottom of the app and we'll do what we can to add it.
    • 
          
  • A webcam is optional, and recommended for the interactive camera scenes. Without one, those scenes play on a demo clip.
