What we bring

A show, not a screensaver.

Camera-reactive generative visuals

Live cameras feed the visuals: the crowd and the performers become part of the picture, in real time.

Fire that follows the performer

Flames, embers and fluid trails that track whoever is on stage. No green screen, no costume, no setup for the artist.

Projection mapping

Stages, sculptures, buildings and tree lines. If it has a surface, we can map it.

LED walls

Content and live output built for your wall's real resolution, from a single panel run to the main stage.

Controller-driven live VJ sets

Played by hand, on a game controller or MIDI, locked to the music. Every set is different because it is performed.

Custom visuals for artists

Show packages built around your music, your brand and your stage plot, ready for your own VJ or for us to run.
