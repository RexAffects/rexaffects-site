Who we are: RexAffects, run by Rex Roberts, a sole proprietor, Fayetteville, AR, United States. Email: support@rexaffects.com. We are the "controller" of the information described here, except where this policy says a company acts on its own account.

This policy covers three things: rexaffects.com (the website), the Plugin Player desktop app, and the RexAffects store (buying scene packs and Resolume plugins, signing in, downloads). It also covers emails we send and the Instagram DM automation we run.

We wrote this in plain English on purpose. If a sentence here reads differently from what we actually do, tell us and we'll fix the sentence or the practice.

1. The short version

  • We collect as little as we can. Most of what the store keeps about you is a scrambled (hashed) copy of your email, not the email itself.
  • The app doesn't watch what you do in it, and it has no ads. The app goes online only to check what you own, to see which scene is free this month, and to look for updates. Nothing leaves your PC from the Feedback or crash panels unless you press SEND, and you can see exactly what is in the report first.
  • Payments are taken by Stripe (its "Sold through Link" service). We never see or store your card number.
  • The website uses the Meta (Facebook) pixel to measure our ads. The app does not.
  • We never sell your information. We never rent it out.
  • You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, to fix it, or to delete it: email support@rexaffects.com.

2.4 Instagram DMs (ManyChat)

If you comment a keyword on one of our Instagram posts, an automation sends you a DM with a link. That automation runs on ManyChat, which holds your Instagram name, id and the messages exchanged, as our processor. We don't ask for your email in the DM. You can stop the automation by not replying, or by blocking the account; ask us and we'll delete the ManyChat contact.

2.5 Emails we send

  • Transactional (always, no opt-in needed): your sign-in code, your purchase email with download links, a plugin-ready notice, a confirmation of a list signup, a reply to your feedback. These come from store@rexaffects.com through Resend.
  • Marketing (only if you opted in): the monthly free scene and occasional news. Every one has an unsubscribe link and our postal address. We honour unsubscribes within 10 business days, usually at once.

3. Why we're allowed to (lawful basis)

For readers in the EU, UK and places with similar rules:

  • Contract: everything needed to sell you a scene, unlock it, deliver the plugin, keep it unlocked and give you support (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Legal obligation: tax and accounting records of sales; proving consent (Art. 6(1)(c)).
  • Legitimate interests: keeping the store and app secure and rate-limited; fixing bugs from reports you send; checking for updates; preventing fraud and abuse of unlocks (Art. 6(1)(f)). You may object (see §7).
  • Consent: the marketing list, SMS, the Meta pixel cookie, and sending feedback or crash reports (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw consent any time; that doesn't undo what happened before.

5. How long we keep things

  • Purchases, refunds, disputes: as long as the purchase stands, and then for the period tax and bookkeeping law requires (in the US, usually 7 years).
  • Account email hash, device records: while you have an account; a device drops off after 180 days of silence; delete the account and all of it goes (see §7).
  • List signup and marketing: until you unsubscribe; then only the consent record (date, text, IP).
  • Feedback and crash reports: deleted once dealt with, at most 12 months.
  • Rate-limit counters and server logs: 30 days at most.
  • Sign-in codes: 15 minutes.
  • Bookings: until the enquiry is closed; deleted on request.

6. Children

Plugin Player and the store are not for children. You must be at least 13 to use the website, the app or to join a list, and at least 18 (or the age of majority where you live) to buy. If you're under 16 in the EU, you need a parent's permission before you sign up to our emails. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13; if we learn we have, we delete it. Parents: email support@rexaffects.com.

7. Your rights, and how to use them

Wherever you live, you can email support@rexaffects.com and ask us to:

  • tell you what we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • fix something that's wrong;
  • delete it ("erasure"). We'll delete your account, devices, feedback, list entry and marketing record. We keep the purchase record itself (hashed email, what you bought, the Stripe ids) because tax law and Stripe's dispute rules require it, and so a refund or chargeback can still be matched to the sale;
  • stop processing based on legitimate interests ("object"), or restrict it while we sort out a dispute;
  • give you your data in a portable form (a plain file);
  • withdraw consent (unsubscribe link, the toggle in the app's SIGN IN tab, or an email).

We answer within 30 days. We'll check it's you (usually by replying to the email on the account). We won't charge for this unless a request is plainly repetitive. You will never be treated worse for using these rights.

EU/EEA and UK. You can also complain to your national data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO, https://ico.org.uk). We are not established in the EU and have not appointed an EU representative under Art. 27 GDPR.

California. We are not a "business" under the CCPA (we are far below its revenue and volume thresholds), but we honour the same requests from Californians anyway: to know, to delete, to correct, and not to be discriminated against. We do not sell personal information. The Meta pixel on the website may count as "sharing" for advertising; if you don't want that, decline the cookie notice or use a browser setting that blocks the pixel. Our website does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals, because there is no agreed standard for them; it does honour Global Privacy Control (GPC) by not loading the pixel.

Other US states, Canada, Australia and elsewhere. The same requests, same address, same 30 days.

8. Security

Payment details never touch our servers. Emails are stored hashed with a salt. Session tokens and sign-in codes are stored hashed. On your PC the app keeps your email and session token encrypted with Windows' built-in protection. The store's API keys are restricted. Logs are stripped of emails, keys and tokens before they are written. No system is perfectly safe; if a breach affects you we will tell you without undue delay, and tell the regulator within 72 hours where that applies.

9. Changes

When we change this policy we'll update the date at the top, keep the old versions and send any of them on request (email support@rexaffects.com), and for a material change we'll email account holders or show a notice in the app before it takes effect.
